A family-run crew from Te Kuiti

Te Kuiti is a small town and word travels fast. Our work has to hold up, because the person whose lawns we mow on Tuesday plays bowls with the person who rings us on Wednesday.

We look after homes, rentals and commercial sites across Te Kuiti, Piopio, Aria, Waitomo and Otorohanga. Before we leave any job we check it over, sweep up and cart the mess away.
